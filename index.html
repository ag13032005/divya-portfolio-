About

The Eye
Behind the
Lens

Divyansh Shaw is a Mumbai-based cinematographer who transforms raw moments into cinematic poetry.

Trained at Whistling Woods International, one of Asia's most prestigious film schools, Divyansh developed a deep mastery of cinematography, camera language, lighting, and cinematic storytelling.

Specializing in short films, music videos, podcasts, commercials and digital productions, he brings an obsessive attention to detail and a director's eye to every frame. He believes every frame should contribute to the story — nothing is accidental, everything is intentional.
